# Minimum Viable Prototype

## Objective
Prove that autonomous strategic warfare creates interesting observable history.

## Required

- 4–6 AI factions.
- 60–150 provinces represented as a graph or grid.
- Ownership and borders.
- Population and industry.
- Manpower and simple equipment stockpile.
- Armies with strength, morale, organization, supply, and location.
- Army movement between adjacent provinces.
- Supply tracing to controlled supply sources.
- Simple battle resolution.
- Territory capture.
- War declarations.
- Ceasefire/peace condition.
- Strategic AI that attacks, defends, reinforces, and retreats.
- Simulation clock with pause and speed controls.
- Event/history log.
- Save/load.
- Basic main window.
- Basic tray icon/menu.

## Explicitly deferred

- Advanced diplomacy.
- Civil wars.
- Named commanders.
- Technology trees.
- Detailed air warfare.
- Naval warfare.
- Espionage.
- Modding.
- Multiplayer.
- Complex procedural geography.
