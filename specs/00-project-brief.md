# Project Brief

## Working title
Endless War Simulator

## Platform
Ubuntu MATE desktop.

## Product statement
A persistent artificial-history war simulation that runs autonomously in the background. The player occasionally intervenes through strategic policies and priorities, while AI factions, governments, commanders, economies, and armies generate the actual history.

## Design pillars

1. **Autonomy** — the world progresses without constant player input.
2. **Strategic influence** — the player steers intent, not individual units.
3. **Emergent history** — memorable wars, leaders, collapses, recoveries, and territorial changes are recorded.
4. **Low attention cost** — the game should coexist with normal desktop work.
5. **Persistence** — closing the main window does not stop the simulation; save/load and offline catch-up are foundational.
6. **Readable complexity** — deep systems should be summarized clearly through fronts, trends, alerts, and history.

## Non-goals for the first prototype

- Real-time tactical combat.
- Detailed 3D graphics.
- Multiplayer.
- Historically accurate real-world nations.
- Hundreds of unit types.
- Complex political ideology simulation.
- Modding support before core systems stabilize.
